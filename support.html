Common questions

I lost my premium subscription after reinstalling

Open the Taskio paywall (Settings → Upgrade to Pro) and tap Restore. As long as you're signed into the same Apple ID that bought the subscription, it will reactivate.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → Taskio Pro → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

In Taskio, go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently erases your account and all your data on Taskio. (Cancelling a subscription is a separate step — see above.)

What languages does Taskio support?

English and Georgian (ქართული). The language follows your iOS system language.
