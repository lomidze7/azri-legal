Common questions

Taskio's Settings open from the profile icon at the top of the Insights tab.

I lost my premium subscription after reinstalling

Open the Taskio paywall (Settings → Upgrade to Pro) and tap Restore. As long as you're signed into the same Apple ID that bought the subscription, it will reactivate.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → Taskio Pro → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

In Taskio, open Settings, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete account and confirm. This permanently erases your account and all your data on Taskio. (Cancelling a subscription is a separate step — see above.)

What languages does Taskio support?

English and Georgian (ქართული). Taskio starts in Georgian in Georgia and in English everywhere else. You can switch any time in Settings → Language.
