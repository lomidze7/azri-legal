Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Azri ("we", "us") collects when you use the Azri mobile application, why we collect it, and how you can delete it. By using Azri you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Azri is a mobile app developed by Giorgi Lomidze, based in Georgia. You can reach us at giolomimi@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

We collect only what we need to run the app. Specifically:

  • Account identifiers (email and name) — provided by Apple when you sign in with Apple. Stored in our backend (Appwrite) so we can identify your account across devices. If you choose Apple's private-relay email, we never see your real address.
  • Subscription state — RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID and your purchase status from Apple. We use this to unlock premium features. We never see your payment details — those stay with Apple.
  • Brain dump text — when you submit text or a transcribed voice note to be parsed into tasks, that text is transmitted to OpenRouter (a third-party AI provider) via our server. The provider processes the request and returns structured tasks. We do not store the raw text on our servers after the response is returned; OpenRouter's own retention is governed by their policy.
  • Diary entries — stored in Appwrite, tied to your account, so they sync to your other devices.
  • Trial timestamp — a single number marking when your 3-day free trial started, stored in your Appwrite account preferences. Used only to determine whether your trial is still active.
  • Anonymous usage counter — a small per-device counter (number of times you've used Azri's AI parsing) is stored keyed by your device's identifier-for-vendor. This is not linked to your identity.

3. Tasks, habits, and other on-device data

Most of your data — including tasks, habits, mood logs, energy logs, and motivation cards — is stored only on your device using MMKV (a local storage library). It never leaves your phone. If you delete the app, that data is gone.

4. Third parties

  • Apple — Apple Sign-In, App Store purchases, push notifications (when enabled).
  • Appwrite — account storage, diary sync, function execution. Hosted at api.kalorio.ge.
  • RevenueCat — manages the link between your Apple subscription and your premium entitlement.
  • OpenRouter — processes the text you submit to the Brain Dump feature and returns parsed tasks.

We do not sell or rent your data to advertisers. We do not use it for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

5. How long we keep it

We retain your account data until you delete your account. The Brain Dump text is not persisted beyond the single request that generates the task list. Anonymous usage counters persist indefinitely on Appwrite but contain no personal information.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the Azri app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This:

  • Permanently erases your Appwrite account (email, name, trial timestamp, diary entries).
  • Deletes your device's anonymous usage counter on our servers.
  • Signs you out of the app.

Your App Store subscription, if any, continues until the end of the current billing period — cancel it separately in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

To delete locally-stored data (tasks, habits, etc.), uninstall the app.

7. Children

Azri is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account.

8. International data transfers

We are based in Georgia. Our Appwrite instance is hosted in Europe. OpenRouter is based in the United States. Apple and RevenueCat operate globally. By using Azri you consent to your data being processed in these jurisdictions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be communicated in-app or via the email tied to your account. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the latest version.
